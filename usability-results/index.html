Usability Testing Results

The primary observation I made during user testing was that users were getting a little lost with the “continue” button, especially if they selected all dialogue options they didn’t think to also press continue. Other than that I observed that the simple starting instructions were enough for users to understand how to navigate through each scene. I also found that users understood the message and story well.

To improve my project I will be changing how the continue button works as well as dimming the interactive circles when they are not active. Adding more interactions and a real concluding scene will strengthen the impact of my story. I also need to continue refining the art to enhance the visual storytelling.
