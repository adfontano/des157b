Proposal

My capstone project will be a narrative based game that explores a future where the climate crisis has had dramatic impacts on day to day life. The target audience for this project is young adults ages 18-30 who are aware of the broader effects of climate change, but are unmotivated to make any changes in their life. It will be a dystopian narrative that explores the impacts of the general population's apathy to creating change. I want to show what continued apathy towards climate change will look like and how a dystopian way of living could become normalized.

I imagine the gameplay will primarily revolve around navigating dialogue trees, where the player can ask questions about the environment or will be prompted to ask others questions, thus learning more about the world they have entered. Right now I’m a little unsure about the best way to implement dialogue trees, there are a couple different libraries I’m looking at but they’re a bit confusing.
