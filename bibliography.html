Annotated Bibliography

“15 Examples of Climate Change in Everyday Life (2025 Guide).” SolarTech, 13 Aug. 2025, solartechonline.com/blog/climate-change-examples-everyday-life/.

Covers the many ways that climate change impacts will change daily life, from traffic delays to outdoor exercise. Its discussion of what daily activities and industries will be forced to change is of particular relevance to my topic. This article specifically combines many different climate change effects that will be felt by different industries and different geographies, offering a good overview of how the world will change. The conclusion the author reaches is that if drastic change is not made daily life will have to evolve to adapt to increased climate disasters, poor health, structural damages, and supply chain disruptions.

Cho, Renée. “How Climate Change Will Alter Our Food.” State of the Planet, 25 July 2018, solartechonline.com/blog climate-change-examples-everyday-life/.

This article covers how each section of the food industry will be impacted by climate change. The reduced production of many food groups such as dairy, vegetables, and fish will impact the cost of groceries and the average diet. The author also covers how some crops may not reduce in yield, but rising co2 levels will lead to reduced nutritional value. The topic of this article is highly relevant to my topic as a reduction in agriculture will impact what we eat every day and the quality of our health.

“FAQ: Climate Change in California.” Scripps Institution of Oceanography, UC San Diego, scripps.ucsd.edu/research/climate-change-resources/faq-climate-change-california. Accessed 13 May 2026.

This article was published by the UC San Diego Scripps institute of Oceanography who are working to protect the planet and find solutions to climate change. It covers the effects of climate change that impact California, from drought to loss of sea life. It discusses how a large percentage of the population will be at risk of flooding and how weather related deaths will increase with intense heat waves. This article is relevant to my topic as I want to place this narrative in a specific location and learning about area specific impacts will be important.
